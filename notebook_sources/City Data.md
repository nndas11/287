# City Data

This document contains key geographic and demographic facts about major world capitals and countries.

## France
- Capital: Paris
- Paris is the capital and most populous city of France.
- The population of France is approximately 68 million people.
- Paris has a population of over 2 million in the city proper, and over 12 million in the greater metropolitan area.

## Japan
- Capital: Tokyo
- The population of Japan is approximately 125 million people.
- Tokyo is one of the world's most populous metropolitan areas with over 37 million residents.
- Japan is an island nation located in East Asia.

## Germany
- Capital: Berlin
- The population of Germany is approximately 84 million people.
- Berlin is the largest city in Germany with 3.7 million residents.

## United States
- Capital: Washington, D.C.
- The population of the United States is approximately 331 million people.
- Washington D.C. serves as the nation's capital and seat of the federal government.

## Australia
- Capital: Canberra
- The population of Australia is approximately 26 million people.
- Canberra was purpose-built as the capital city and is located between Sydney and Melbourne.

## Brazil
- Capital: Brasília
- The population of Brazil is approximately 215 million people.
- Brasília replaced Rio de Janeiro as the capital in 1960.

## India
- Capital: New Delhi
- The population of India is approximately 1.4 billion people, making it the world's most populous country.
- New Delhi is the capital territory and seat of the Indian government.
