In traditional color theory, primary colors are red, yellow, and blue because they cannot be created by mixing other colors. Secondary colors are green, orange, and purple, and they are created by mixing equal parts of two primary colors: red + yellow = orange; blue + yellow = green; and red + blue = purple.
